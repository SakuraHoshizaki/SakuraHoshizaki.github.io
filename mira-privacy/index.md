# Privacy Policy for Mira Voice

Last updated: May 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Mira Voice handles user data.

## Developer

Developer: [SakuraAoi]  
Contact: [liupp55@gmail.com]

## Summary

Mira Voice is a voice feedback app. The app records audio through the device microphone and analyzes the voice on the user's device to provide feedback such as a gender perception score and related voice feedback.

Mira Voice does not transmit audio recordings, analysis results, or any other user data to our servers.

## Data Accessed by the App

Mira Voice may access the following data on the user's device:

- Microphone audio, when the user chooses to record audio in the app
- Locally generated analysis results, such as voice feedback scores

This data is used only to provide the app's voice analysis and feedback features.

## Data Collection and Sharing

Mira Voice does not collect user data from the device.

Specifically:

- Audio recordings are not uploaded to our servers.
- Voice analysis results are not uploaded to our servers.
- The app does not use analytics services.
- The app does not send crash reports or error logs.
- The app does not require account registration.
- The app does not sell or share user data with third parties.

## Local Data Storage

Any audio or analysis data used by the app remains on the user's device. Users can delete locally stored data by using the app's available deletion functions, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app.

## Data Security

Because Mira Voice does not transmit user data to our servers, user audio and analysis results are processed locally on the device. Users should still protect their own device with appropriate security settings such as a screen lock.

## Children's Privacy

Mira Voice is not directed to children. The app is intended for general users who want voice feedback.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app's features change. If the app begins to transmit, collect, or share user data in the future, this policy and the Google Play Data safety information will be updated accordingly.
